Office Student Hours

Student hours are set times dedicated to all of you. Most professors call these “office hours” - which I always found too formal and scary!

Why student hours?

Students often think that “office hours” are times when professors should be left alone - which is the total opposite. I’m hoping that by calling them student hours, it might alleviate this misunderstanding.

This means that during these times I’ll be waiting for you to talk to me where you can ask whatever questions you may have.

You can schedule a meeting with me here.

Or just knock on the door and pop in!

ST303 - Linear Models

What we will cover

This modules aims to introduction you to the fundamentals of linear models This course will cover (but is not limited to) the following:

  • Statistical ideas
  • Mathematical models
  • Real data
  • R demonstrations
  • Short activities and questions
  • Case studies

The important skill is knowing how to interpret and evaluate a linear model.

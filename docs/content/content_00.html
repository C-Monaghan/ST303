Welcome to ST303

Module overview

Welcome to third year and welcome to ST303!

We will be covering lots of things related to linear models. This will range from the simple and multiple linear regression, ordinary and weighted least squares, inference, and diagnostics (among other things).

Learning outcomes

On successful completion of the module, you should be able to

  • Fit and interpret multiple regression models.
  • Understand the theory of multiple regression.
  • Assess model assumptions.
  • Use model diagnostics.

Assessment

This module is broken down as 25% continuous assessment and 75% official exam.

  • 4 assignments in the form of a Moodle quiz (worth 10%)
  • 1 mid-term exam (worth 15%)
  • 1 university exam (worth 75%)
NoteThe dates for each exam are still being finalised
ImportantDetails for Erasmus students

If you are an Erasmus student here for Semester 1 only (September - December), you will not be here for the final exam. With that in mind, your assignments and mid-term will be worth more marks.

If you are here for the full academic year (semester 1 AND semester 2), then your grades will follow the same breakdown as listed above.

There are many little hidden things you can do with these slides.

Finally, if you want to make a PDF of these slides you can press e, followed by ctrl + p.
